INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION OF THE PROPOSED n=4e FAMILY
2026-10-09. Construction/proof only; novelty is not established.

1. Binary coefficient spaces

Identify Q=Alt(4,F2) with F2^6 using lex pairs 01,02,03,12,13,23.
Write a vector as (a,b,c,d,f,g). Its Pfaffian quadratic form is
q(a,b,c,d,f,g)=ag+bf+cd, with polar pairing
beta(X,Y)=q(X+Y)+q(X)+q(Y).

Let V be the four-dimensional subspace
A(u,v,w,z)=(u+v,u,w,z,v,u+v).
Then q(A)=u+v+uv+wz has exactly six zeros: three with u=v=0
and wz=0, and three with (u,v) nonzero and w=z=1.
Only one of these six vectors is zero. The map from (u,v,w,z)
is injective, directly from entries b,f,c,d.

Let O be spanned by bitmasks 19 and 33, and H by 12 and 22.
In displayed coordinates:
19=(1,1,0,0,1,0), 33=(1,0,0,0,0,1),
12=(0,0,1,1,0,0), 22=(0,1,1,0,1,0).
For both spaces, q of a combination with coefficients (s,t) is
s+t+st, so each is an anisotropic binary plane.

Directly, beta(A,19)=(u+v)+u+v=0 and
beta(A,33)=(u+v)+(u+v)=0. The pairing beta is nondegenerate
on the six-dimensional space Q, so dim O^perp=4 and V=O^perp.
Both generators of H belong to V (labels 12 and 7 respectively),
so H is orthogonal to O. [Here A label is u+2v+4w+8z.]

2. Extension pencil

Fix any integer e>=1 and E=F_(2^e). Choose alpha with degree e
so 1,alpha,...,alpha^(e-1) is a binary basis. (For e=1 choose
alpha=1; only alpha^0 is used.) Set X_0=A in V. For 1<=j<e,
choose X_j in O if j is odd and in H if j is even. Define

K=A+sum_(j=1)^(e-1) alpha^j X_j in Alt(4,E).

The binary parameter space has dimension 4+2(e-1)=2e+2.
It maps injectively into Alt(4,E): if K=0, coefficientwise
independence of 1,alpha,...,alpha^(e-1) implies every X_j=0.

The Pfaffian is the scalar extension of q, hence
q(K)=sum_j alpha^(2j)q(X_j)
     +sum_(i<j) alpha^(i+j)beta(X_i,X_j).

If i+j is odd, the cross pairing vanishes. For i=0 this follows
from V=O^perp; for positive i,j it follows from O perpendicular H.
If i+j is even, write i+j=2k. Then i<k<j, or at least 0<=k<j
when i=0; in either case k<j.

Suppose some X_j with j>=1 is nonzero, and let m be the largest
such index. In the expansion in 1,alpha^2,...,alpha^(2(e-1)),
the coefficient at index m is q(X_m)=1 by anisotropy. No cross
term can reach this index, because all their indices k are strictly
below the larger active index, which is at most m. No diagonal term
with index above m is nonzero. The displayed squared powers are
linearly independent: a binary relation among them is the square
of the same relation among 1,alpha,...,alpha^(e-1), and Frobenius
is injective. Therefore q(K) is nonzero.

Conversely, if all X_j=0 for j>=1, q(K)=q(A). We have proved
q(K)=0 iff all extension parameters vanish and q(A)=0.
There are exactly six singular matrices in this entire pencil.

3. Trace passage and ranks

View E^4 as a binary vector space of dimension n=4e. Define
T_K(x,y)=Tr_(E/F2)(x^T K y). This is a binary alternating form.
The finite-field trace pairing is nondegenerate. For completeness,
Tr(z)=z+z^2+...+z^(2^(e-1)) is a nonzero polynomial of degree
2^(e-1)<2^e, so is not identically zero on E. Since its values
are binary, some z has trace 1. Given c != 0, d=z/c gives
Tr(cd)=1. This works for even e as well; one must not rely on
Tr(1)=1 outside odd-degree extensions.

If Kx=0 then x lies in the radical of T_K. Conversely, if Kx
is nonzero, use a nonzero coordinate and the nondegenerate trace
pairing to choose y with T_K(x,y)=1. Thus the radical of T_K
is the E-kernel of K, viewed over F2. In particular,
rank_F2(T_K)=e*rank_E(K).

The zero parameter produces rank zero. The other five singular A
are nonzero alternating 4x4 matrices with vanishing Pfaffian, hence
rank 2 over F2 and over E; they give binary rank 2e. Every other
parameter gives binary rank 4e. The trace map is injective on all
alternating E-matrices by the same trace-pairing argument, so this
binary pencil still has parameter dimension 2e+2.

4. Quadratic map and component count

Choose any binary basis of E^4, and let M_0,...,M_(2e+1) be
the binary matrices of a basis of the trace pencil. Define
f_k(x)=sum_(a<b) (M_k)ab x_a x_b for 0<=k<2e+2,
and append 2e-2 zero coordinates to obtain f:F2^(4e)->F2^(4e).
The padding number is nonnegative for every e>=1, including e=1.

A Boolean quadratic function is bent iff its polar form is
nondegenerate. Exactly six effective component masks are nonbent,
including zero. Every effective mask has 2^(2e-2) full-mask lifts.
Consequently the number of nonzero nonbent components is

6*2^(2e-2)-1 = 3*2^(2e-1)-1 = 3*2^(n/2-1)-1.

Thus this construction meets that numerical target for every
positive n divisible by four. Any assertion that this is a globally
sharp bound depends on the separately supplied lower-bound theorem.
No claim concerning n=2 mod 4 follows from this argument.

5. Independent finite checks

verify_family.py checks all 2^(2e+2) parameter vectors for e=1..6
using finite-field multiplication reduced modulo explicitly validated
irreducible polynomials. In every case the only singular labels are
0,4,8,13,14,15. The corresponding nonzero nonbent counts are
5,23,95,383,1535,6143 for n=4,8,12,16,20,24.
The original n=12 full binary trace certificate is preserved separately.
