THE ESTABLISHED CYCLIC-SEMIFIELD CORE AND THE COMPONENT-COUNT COROLLARY
9 October 2026

Historical scope
----------------
The entire alternating pencil in INFINITE_FAMILY_PROOF.txt is congruent to a
hyperbolic binary plane plus an established Jha-Johnson cyclic-semifield core.
It is not a new underlying construction. The precise extremal Boolean
component-count consequence was not located as a published theorem. That
limited search result does not establish novelty or publication priority.
Any potentially new contribution is the application/corollary and formal
verification. The correspondence below is mathematical; it changes none of
the frozen Lean sources or their certified statements.

Fixed congruence
----------------
Encode an alternating 4 by 4 matrix by its six upper entries in order
01,02,03,12,13,23. Binary masks list these bits least-significant first.
Let
    P = [[1,1,1,0], [0,1,1,1], [1,0,1,0], [0,1,0,1]].
Its columns have binary masks 5,11,7,10 and its determinant is one.
The congruence M -> transpose(P) M P maps the following ordered bases:
    H: (12,22) -> (18,28)
    O: (19,33) -> (22,26)
    D: (47,61) -> (1,32).
The original base space V equals D direct-sum H. In the cross block between
coordinates (0,1) and (2,3), the images of H and O are respectively (I,R)
and (J,JR), where
    R = [[0,1],[1,1]],    J = [[1,1],[0,1]].
Here R^2+R+I=0, J^2=I and JR=R^2 J.

Let E=GF(2^e), and let alpha have degree e. Write K=span_F2{I,R}, a matrix
field isomorphic to GF(4). The original base parameters (u,v,w,z) become
    x=u+w+z, y=v+w+z, h0=u+v+z, h1=w+z.
This binary parameter change is invertible. With T=alpha J and Z=[[0,1],[1,0]],
the transformed pencil consists exactly of
    M(x,y,B) = [[xZ, B], [transpose(B), yZ]],
    x,y in F2,
    B = h0 I + h1 R
        + sum_(1<=j<e) alpha^j J^(j mod 2) (s_j I+t_j R).
Thus the cross-block core is S=sum_(0<=j<e) T^j K, and the two other binary
directions form the hyperbolic plane D. Set gamma=alpha^2; T^2=gamma I.
For e=1 there are no extension terms; alpha=gamma=1 may be chosen for this
interpretation irrespective of the unused generator choice in the formal proof.

Identification with established machinery
----------------------------------------
The K action makes E^2 an e-dimensional K-vector space. T is semilinear for
the nontrivial automorphism of K. For e>1, gamma generates E over F2, so the
algebra generated by K,T contains E and J. The matrices I,R,J,JR are linearly
independent over E, so that algebra is all Mat_2(E). A T-invariant K-subspace
would therefore be invariant under Mat_2(E), and must be zero or E^2.
For e=1 irreducibility is automatic in K-dimension one. Consequently S is
exactly the irreducible-semilinear Jha-Johnson construction recalled in [1].
For odd e its norm/power-basis specialization is also described in [2].
Reference [2] is not being asserted to cover even e.

Determinant-one fibre
--------------------
Split even and odd powers:
    B=h(gamma)+T o(gamma),
    h(X)=sum_(2j<e) C_(2j) X^j,
    o(X)=sum_(2j+1<e) C_(2j+1) X^j,    C_j in K.
A bar denotes coefficientwise conjugation C -> C^2 in K; X is fixed.
Direct 2 by 2 determinant expansion gives
    det B = f(gamma),
    f(X)=h(X) bar(h)(X)+X o(X) bar(o)(X) in F2[X].
If h is nonzero, the first term has even degree 2 deg h and leading
coefficient one. If o is nonzero, the second term has odd degree 2 deg o+1
and leading coefficient one. The leading degrees cannot cancel. Both are
less than e, so evaluation at gamma is injective on these binary polynomials.
Thus det B=0 forces h=o=0. Also det B=1 forces o=0 and h a nonzero constant.
There are exactly three such constants. This includes e=1 directly.
For even e the displayed determinant identity is a polynomial identity with
coefficientwise conjugation. No quadratic field extension E(K)/E or its
field norm is assumed.

The Pfaffian of M(x,y,B) is xy+det B. There are three zero-Pfaffian cases
with B=0 and xy=0, and three with det B=1 and x=y=1. Hence exactly six forms
are singular. Trace descent, the quadratic bent criterion and output padding
give 6*2^(2e-2)-1 = 3*2^(2e-1)-1 nonzero nonbent masks in dimension 4e.
The universal lower bound remains an external cited theorem.

Reproducible check
------------------
Run python3 family/verify_correspondence.py from the source directory.
It verifies the binary congruence on every alternating matrix, the exact
parameter transformation, the matrix-field identities, and determinant-zero
and determinant-one fibres for every core parameter at e=1,...,6. These finite
checks supplement the preceding proof; they do not replace the uniform Lean
certificate or prove historical novelty.
