# Primary-source context and scope

Checked on 12 October 2026. This is a bounded source audit, not an exhaustive priority determination.

1. Joseph O'Rourke, Prismatoid Band-Unfolding Revisited, arXiv:2603.09813v3, revised 18 September 2026.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09813v3
   https://arxiv.org/html/2603.09813v3
   See Section 5 / PDF page 14, Proposition 9, and its use in Lemma 8. The certificate addresses the unrestricted center-containment wording. It identifies a dependency gap, not a disproof of the later theorem. The inspected v3 HTML and PDF agreed; no later correction was found in the bounded search.

2. Joseph O'Rourke, An Addendum to the Paper: Edge-Unfolding Nearly Flat Convex Caps, arXiv:1709.02433.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.02433
   https://arxiv.org/html/1709.02433v1
   This source distinguishes a small-angle weighted-center approximation from the true rotation center, and allows the latter outside the hull. Its printed quantitative formula has an inconsistency, so this package relies only on that contextual distinction and not on its quantitative estimate.

3. Tristan Needham, Visual Complex Analysis, printed pp.20–21.
   Oxford publisher preview:
   https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780192695451_A46510629/preview-9780192695451_A46510629.pdf
   The classical two-center composition formula was checked directly in this preview. Composition algebra and the elementary counterexample are not novelty claims.

4. Dürer's edge-unfolding problem, maintained primary problem list:
   https://topp.openproblem.net/p9
   The general question concerns actual-edge spanning-tree cuts in the original metric. The present planar estimate does not produce such a cut or certify face non-overlap.

No matching exact diameter theorem was found in the bounded searches, including searches for composite rotation centers, convex-hull distance, diameter, and tangent bounds. Negative search results do not establish novelty. The stronger fixed-angle-list constant and directional-width refinement from the mathematical audit are not part of this package's checked theorem claims.
