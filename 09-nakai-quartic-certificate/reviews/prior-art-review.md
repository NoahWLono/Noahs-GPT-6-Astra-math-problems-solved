# Prior-art and scope review

Review cutoff: 12 October 2026. This is a bounded primary-source comparison, not an exhaustive novelty certificate.

## Exact operator overlap

Diethorn, Jeffries, Miller, Packauskas, Pollitz, Rahmati and Vassiliadou, *Resolutions of differential operators of low order for an isolated hypersurface singularity*, arXiv:2209.13110v3, 16 November 2023, Sections 4.1.3 and 4.5, equations (33), (35), Theorem 4.5.1:

https://arxiv.org/html/2209.13110v3

For three variables, their matrix gives the same Hessian-adjugate symbol. Conversion from divided partials and inclusion of the first-order correction give precisely this paper's operator divided by `(d-1)^2`. The formula in three variables is prior art. Their introduction also discusses Vigue's earlier ordinary-homogeneous three-variable result. Neither the three-variable conclusion nor that formula is claimed as new.

## Recent weighted-homogeneous work and the full-word distinction

Yau, Zhu and Zuo, *Nakai conjecture for isolated weighted homogeneous hypersurface singularities*, arXiv:2604.24508v4, 8 October 2026:

https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24508v4

The introduction uses generation order by order, and the principal result is formulated as `der^2(A) != Der^2(A)`. Its cofactor construction overlaps the present method. Excluding a second-order operator from the module generated by derivations and products of two derivations does not on its own rule out cancellations among arbitrary longer words in the full generated algebra. The fixed-quartic certificate explicitly proves exclusion from that full algebra using a preserved ideal.

This scope distinction does not establish that the fixed example or the written higher-dimensional argument is historically new.

## Existing separation methods and known classes

Brumatti and Veloso, *A note on Nakai's conjecture for the ring K[X1,...,Xn]/(a1 X1^m+...+an Xn^m)*, Colloquium Mathematicum 123 (2011), 277-283, DOI 10.4064/cm123-2-10:

https://doi.org/10.4064/cm123-2-10

Stable-ideal separation is already used in the Fermat-type setting. The general strategy is not claimed as original.

Traves, *Nakai's conjecture for varieties smoothed by normalization*, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society 127 (1999), 2245-2248:

https://www.usna.edu/Users/math/traves/_files/documents/nakaisconjecture.pdf

This proves the full conjecture for its smooth-normalization class. Known full-ring cases must be distinguished from second-order-only formulations.

## Explicit classical import in the written extension

Eisenbud and Ulrich, *Duality and socle generators for residual intersections*, Journal fur die reine und angewandte Mathematik 756 (2019), 183-226, Theorem 7.1, page 210:

https://www.math.purdue.edu/~bulrich/papers/published%20version%20of%20Duality%20for%20Residual%20Intersections.pdf

The written ordinary-homogeneous theorem uses the characteristic-zero zero-dimensional complete-intersection Jacobian-socle result. Its application is explained in the paper. This classical theorem is not imported as an unproved Lean axiom: the fixed quartic instead proves its required nonmembership by a concrete coefficient functional.

## Unresolved originality questions

The mixed quartic has a written isolatedness proof and a written obstruction to a linear Thom-Sebastiani splitting. These do not classify it up to analytic or contact equivalence, do not exclude every previously known theorem, and do not prove that it belongs to a new class. The source review is not exhaustive over all older Nakai literature. No priority claim is made for the higher-dimensional construction, the full-word identity, the example, or the formalization.

The documented contribution of this package is its explicit, reproducible Lean certificate and exact mathematical scope. It does not resolve the unrestricted conjecture.
