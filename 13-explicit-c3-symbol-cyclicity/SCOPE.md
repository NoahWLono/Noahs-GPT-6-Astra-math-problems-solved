# Exact mathematical and formal scope

Let K be a characteristic-zero field, A a division ring with a K-algebra structure, and q a primitive fifth root of unity in K. `PrimeFiveFamily.SymbolFamily K A` supplies units U,V with UV = q VU and scalar fifth powers x,y; an actual K-basis indexed by `(Fin 5 × Fin 5)` whose vectors are U^i V^j; and the assertion that the center consists exactly of the scalar image.

It also supplies a ring automorphism sigma of A and a ring automorphism tau of K. The action is semilinear, sigma cubed is the identity, sigma(U)=V, sigma(V)=(UV)^(-1), and tau fixes q. These are explicit input hypotheses. The fixed field and fixed division algebra are the actual fixed objects for tau and sigma, not arbitrary spaces carrying desired conclusions.

For every such family, the checked Laurent numerator N is fixed and nonscalar. Its exact fifth power is scalar, by the 69-term evaluated certificate and 75-term support alignment. The final export proves that the commutative subalgebra generated by N over the fixed scalar field has dimension five, is Galois, has cyclic Galois group, and is maximal among commutative subalgebras of the fixed algebra. No extra premise assuming N^5 is scalar or assuming cyclicity remains.
