# Prior-art boundary

Historical novelty is unestablished. The bounded comparison in the paper did not identify the exact root-free, specialization-uniform theorem or exact numerator. A negative literature search does not establish priority.

Fryer's invariant-generator construction is explicitly credited: [The q-Division Ring and its Fixed Rings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.5071v2), especially Proposition 3.3 and Theorem 3.4. The present cyclicity theorem is not a full fixed-ring isomorphism result and does not attribute a published cube-root-removal question to Fryer's Questions 4.12.

[Reichstein and Vonessen](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408420v2), Section 15, discuss a related monomial action inside a larger group; invariance under that whole group is a different requirement. [Matzri's dihedral degree-five theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.0340) requires a specific splitting-field hypothesis not supplied merely by this order-three action. The [Auel–Brussel–Garibaldi–Vishne survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.3304v2) gives historical context.

The paper records the detailed distinctions and exact bibliography versions. No unrestricted cyclicity problem is resolved by this package.
